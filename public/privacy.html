Loot Goblin Privacy Policy

Developer: Adrian Chapman • Effective Date: October 2026

We respect your privacy and practice data minimization. We only collect the minimal information needed to operate item identification, manage subscriptions, and maintain account integrity. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Authentication: Encrypted email and credentials handled via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Item Images: Images submitted for valuation are securely processed via Google Gemini AI models strictly to identify the item. Photos are processed ephemerally and are not permanently retained on our servers.
  • Device Tokens: Randomized installation identifiers used to enforce scan credit rate limits and prevent abuse.

2. Third-Party Services

We only share data with service providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, cloud configuration, and analytics.
  • Google Gemini AI: Image understanding and metadata extraction.
  • Apple StoreKit: In-App purchase validation. Payment information is processed solely by Apple and never seen by Loot Goblin.

3. Account & Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

You have full authority to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

1. Open Loot Goblin and go to Settings.

2. Select Delete Account & Data.

3. Confirm deletion to permanently erase your profile and records.

You may also email us directly at lootgoblin.support@gmail.com to request immediate manual deletion.

4. Support & Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact: lootgoblin.support@gmail.com
