Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases or Founder Pass?

If you reinstalled the app or set up a new device, open Loot Goblin and go to Settings > Restore Purchases. The app will communicate with Apple StoreKit to immediately restore your active subscription.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed securely via your Apple ID account:

  1. Open Settings on your iOS device.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Loot Goblin and choose Cancel Subscription.

How do I permanently delete my account and data?

In compliance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1, you can permanently delete your account at any time:

  1. Open Loot Goblin and tap Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  3. Confirm deletion. Your credentials and cloud history will be permanently wiped.

You may also email us directly at lootgoblin.support@gmail.com to request manual account purging.
