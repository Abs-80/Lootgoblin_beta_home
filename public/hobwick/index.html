Hobwick

Record what you own, room by room, so an insurance claim is easier if the worst happens.

What it does

  • Walk through your home room by room, with a checklist of what to record in each.
  • Snap a photo and Hobwick suggests the brand, model and a replacement cost. You confirm what's saved.
  • Keep receipts, serial numbers, warranty and return dates with each item.
  • Reminders for smoke and CO alarm replacement, warranties and return windows.
  • Checks your items against Health Canada's recall list, on your phone.
  • Export reports and spreadsheets for your insurer. English and French.

Private by design

Your inventory stays on your phone. Only the photos you choose to scan are sent off it, to identify the item, and they aren't kept. No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Read the privacy policy.
