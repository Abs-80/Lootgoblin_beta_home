Hobwick Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 • Developer: Adrian Chapman, Canada

Hobwick helps you record what you own, room by room, for insurance. This policy explains what leaves your phone, why, and what you can do about it. Hobwick is made by one Canadian developer, and this page is written in plain language on purpose.

The short version

  • Your inventory is saved on your phone, not on our server: rooms, items, prices, notes, photos and receipts.
  • When you scan an item, its photos are sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI to identify it. We don't keep the photos after the answer comes back.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account in the app, and that also wipes the inventory on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Everything you record: rooms and checklists, item names, brands, models, serial numbers, quantities, dates, prices, notes, photos, receipt photos, warranty and return dates, and your policy contents limit. We can't see any of it. If you lose your phone without a backup, we can't recover it, so please use Export everything in Settings.

Photo locations (GPS) are removed before a photo is saved. If the location can't be removed, the photo isn't saved.

The optional app lock uses your phone's own Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode. We never see these.

Hobwick keeps inventory photos in its own storage. It does not scan, edit or delete your photo library. You can choose photos with the system picker. Saving an additional copy to your photo library is optional and off by default; location metadata is removed from saved copies.

Your complete inventory is not uploaded to our server. Scanning sends only the photos and item details you choose for that scan. A label in a photo may include a serial number or other personal information: check what you select before allowing AI scanning. You can add items manually without AI.

Anonymous usage counts (beta, optional, off unless you turn them on)

During the beta you can choose to share simple counts with us: how many rooms you've finished, items added, reports and exports made, and days the app was opened. They're sent with a random ID that isn't linked to your account or email, and they never include item names, photos, values or anything you typed. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

Diagnostics and crash reports

Hobwick keeps technical diagnostic logs on this phone, limited to about 1 MB of retained files. Records older than 14 days are removed at startup. They exclude inventory details, photos, values and sign-in tokens. They are not uploaded automatically and are excluded from inventory exports and cloud backups. Deleting your account clears them.

This iOS build uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports and technical app and device information to Google so we can fix crashes. We do not intentionally attach inventory records or photos to crash reports. Crash reports are handled under Firebase's data-retention policies. This build does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Performance. Optional beta usage counts are separate and remain off unless you enable them.

Your own backups

You can save inventory backups and reports in your own iCloud Drive, managed by Apple. Signing in to Hobwick on another phone does not transfer the inventory automatically: restore a backup. Your inventory belongs to this phone and stays here when you sign out; another account on this phone sees the same inventory. Deleting your Hobwick account does not delete exported copies or backups in your own iCloud Drive.

Notifications

Reminders (smoke and CO alarm replacement, warranties, return windows) are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved, and they only work if you allow notifications.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information. We use these service providers only to run the app: Google Firebase (sign-in and crash reports), Railway (our server), Google Gemini (identifying items), Open Library (book metadata), and Apple (your optional iCloud Drive backups). When you share a report or export, it goes where you choose (email, Files, Google Drive and so on); we never see it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in Hobwick, open Settings and choose Delete Account & Data. This deletes your sign-in, our records linked to your account ID, and the inventory, photos and receipts on your phone. The app offers an export first.
  • Access or correct: your inventory is on your phone, where you can view, edit, export or delete it.
  • Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA, and Quebec's Law 25 where it applies) you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us at the address below.

Children

Hobwick isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change what leaves your phone, we'll update this page and tell you in the app before it happens.

Contact

Questions or requests: lootgoblin.support@gmail.com (the developer's shared support address). Person responsible for privacy: Adrian Chapman.
